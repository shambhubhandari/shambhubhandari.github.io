Computational Materials Physics & AI

I am completing my PhD in Computational Materials Physics under Prof. Dario Alfè at University College London (thesis submitted), specializing in first-principles calculations, atomistic modelling, machine learning, and agentic AI systems for autonomous materials discovery. My research develops ab initio methodologies for high-pressure binary alloy phase equilibria and melting curves, integrating DFT, molecular dynamics, and machine-learned interatomic potentials with thermodynamic free-energy calculations. I model material defects, electrides, and photocatalysts via Gibbs free-energy reaction pathways. Furthermore, I compute electronic, structural, mechanical, magnetic, optical, and quantum-transport phenomena via DFT and NEGF formalisms.

Selected Publication Highlights

THRUST 01 · HIGH-PRESSURE PHASE EQUILIBRIA Figure 1 of 7

Ab Initio High-Pressure Phase Diagrams of Al–Mg Alloys in the Low Solute Limit

Fully ab initio DFT and thermodynamic free-energy integration mapping the dilute-limit Al–Mg binary phase diagram up to 150 GPa. Uncovers a pressure-driven partitioning reversal above ~60 GPa where Al transitions from liquid-favouring to solid-favouring during the Mg hcp-to-bcc transformation, inverting the coexistence field topology.
